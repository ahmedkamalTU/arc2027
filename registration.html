Policies

Cancellation & Invoicing

Cancellation policy

The cancellation deadline and any administrative fee will be published together with the registration form. A registered participant will be able to nominate a substitute at no penalty by notifying the organizers in advance.

Invoicing

Registration will be confirmed upon payment. Invoices will be issued after payment, with the option to invoice a person or an institution — details will follow together with the registration form.
